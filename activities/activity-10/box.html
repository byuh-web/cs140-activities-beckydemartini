Island Hiking Essentials

Simple tips for safer hikes on Oʻahu.

Trails Gear Safety

What to Bring

Hiking on the island can change quickly because weather and sunlight can feel intense. Bringing water, snacks, and sun protection makes the hike more comfortable and helps you stay focused. Even short trails can take longer than expected, so it’s smart to prepare before you go.

Good shoes help you avoid slipping on mud and loose rocks, especially after rain. Tell someone where you’re going and start earlier in the day so you are not hiking near dark. If conditions look unsafe, turning around is a good decision and still counts as a successful hike.

Quick tip: Pack more water than you think you need and drink regularly.

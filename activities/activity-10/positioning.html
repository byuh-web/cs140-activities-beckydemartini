What to Bring

Hiking on the island can change quickly because weather and sunlight can feel intense. Safety Expectations

Good shoes help you avoid slipping on mud and loose rocks, especially after rain. Tell someone where you’re going and start earlier in the day so you are not hiking near dark. If conditions look unsafe, turning around is a good decision and still counts as a successful hike.
